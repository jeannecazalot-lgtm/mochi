**Description**:
In most couples, one person keeps track of everything: groceries, laundry, the pediatrician appointment, the birthday gift. Mindpair shares that mental load, and even makes it fun.

PLAN IT TOGETHER
List everything that's spinning in your heads, each on your own phone. What one of you checks shows up live for the other.

SPLIT IT FAIRLY
Mochi divides the tasks based on your availability, what you enjoy and what you'd rather avoid. The Balance shows at a glance who's carrying what.

NO MORE NAGGING
Each of you gets your own tasks and reminders. Nobody has to be the one who keeps asking.

RAISE MOCHI TOGETHER
Every task you check off feeds Mochi and earns pearls. Decorate his room, dress him up, and watch him grow week after week.

MOCHI+
The essentials are free. Mochi+ adds a week that's ready to go (your tasks come back on their own), exclusive items and outfits, the Planner's month view and a shared budget. One payment covers the whole household: the 1-Year Pass (one-time payment, nothing renews) or the monthly subscription (renews automatically, cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings).
